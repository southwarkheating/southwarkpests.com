Our standard visit

ServicePrice
Standard pest control visit (homes)£90
Follow-up visits, proofing and larger jobsQuoted and agreed before work continues
Commercial contracts and blocks of flatsQuoted after a survey

A standard visit covers a visit to inspect the problem and begin treatment where appropriate. Many pest problems are solved within that visit or with a small number of follow-ups. Where more work is needed, such as several follow-up visits, proofing, access equipment or treating a large property, we explain exactly what is involved and agree the price with you before we continue.

PestWhat to expect
Wasp nestUsually one visit
FleasUsually one treatment, occasionally a second
MiceFirst visit plus follow-ups to check and clear

Payment

We confirm your booking by phone or email. Payment details are explained when we confirm. We will never add extra work to your bill without agreeing it first.

Landlords, agents and businesses

We can invoice landlords and letting agents directly, and provide written reports. For restaurants, shops, offices and blocks of flats, we quote after a survey. See commercial pest control.

Questions and answers

Is the £90 a fixed price?

It is the price of a standard visit. If your problem needs more, we tell you what and how much before continuing.

Do you charge for a quote?

Commercial and proofing quotes usually follow a survey. Ask us when you get in touch.

Are there extra charges for evenings or weekends?

If a visit outside normal hours costs more, we will tell you before booking it.
