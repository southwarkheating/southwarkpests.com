Local pest control in Brixton

Brixton is made up largely of Victorian terraces, many converted into flats, and large estates. Brixton Market's food stalls and restaurants, the railway arches and busy estates mean rats, mice and cockroaches are frequent problems.

Brixton is in the London Borough of Lambeth. Brixton Village and Market and the O2 Academy are at the heart of the area.

Do you cover Brixton?

How much does pest control cost in Brixton?

How quickly can you get to Brixton?

We aim to see urgent problems, such as rats indoors or a wasp nest by a door, as quickly as we can. Call us and we will tell you when we can attend.

Why do flats in Brixton keep getting mice?

Mice move between flats through shared floor voids, risers and pipe boxing. Treating neighbouring flats together and sealing gaps in each one works far better than treating one flat alone.

Do you treat restaurants and shops in Brixton?

Yes. We work with food businesses, shops and offices, with written reports for your food safety records. See commercial pest control.

Who do I report rats to in Brixton?

If rats are coming from neglected land or a neighbouring property, you can report it to Lambeth Council's environmental health team. For rats in your own home or business, we can inspect and treat.

Do you work with landlords and letting agents in Brixton?

Yes. We arrange access with tenants, treat and proof, and send a written report. See pest control for landlords.
