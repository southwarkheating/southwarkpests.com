Local pest control in Lambeth North

Lambeth North is made up largely of mansion blocks, estates and terraced streets with many rented flats. Rented flats and busy estates see mice, bed bugs and cockroaches, and the park by the Imperial War Museum brings squirrels and foxes.

Lambeth North is in the London Borough of Lambeth, named after its Bakerloo line station. The Imperial War Museum stands in Geraldine Mary Harmsworth Park nearby.

How much does pest control cost in Lambeth North?

How quickly can you get to Lambeth North?

We aim to see urgent problems, such as rats indoors or a wasp nest by a door, as quickly as we can. Call us and we will tell you when we can attend.

Why do flats in Lambeth North keep getting mice?

Mice move between flats through shared floor voids, risers and pipe boxing. Treating neighbouring flats together and sealing gaps in each one works far better than treating one flat alone.

Who do I report rats to in Lambeth North?

If rats are coming from neglected land or a neighbouring property, you can report it to Lambeth Council's environmental health team. For rats in your own home or business, we can inspect and treat.

Yes. We arrange access with tenants, treat and proof, and send a written report. See pest control for landlords.
