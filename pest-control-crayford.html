Pest control in Crayford: questions and answers

Do you cover Crayford?

Yes. We cover Crayford (DA1) and the surrounding streets from our base at South Dock Marina in SE16. Call 020 8050 2022 or book online.

How much does pest control cost in Crayford?

A standard visit is £90, the same as everywhere we work. If you need follow-up visits or proofing, we explain the work and agree the price before we continue. See our prices.

How quickly can you get to Crayford?

We aim to see urgent problems, such as rats indoors or a wasp nest by a door, as quickly as we can. Call us and we will tell you when we can attend.

Can you deal with squirrels or wasps at my house in Crayford?

Yes. We treat wasp nests, usually in one visit, and deal with grey squirrels in lofts legally and humanely before proofing the roof.

Why are there so many rats near the river in Crayford?

The river wall, docks and old drain runs give rats food, water and shelter. Most rat problems in riverside homes start in defective drains, so we check drains and fit rat blockers where suitable.

Who do I report rats to in Crayford?

If rats are coming from neglected land or a neighbouring property, you can report it to Bexley Council's environmental health team. For rats in your own home or business, we can inspect and treat.

Do you work with landlords and letting agents in Crayford?

Yes. We arrange access with tenants, treat and proof, and send a written report. See pest control for landlords.
