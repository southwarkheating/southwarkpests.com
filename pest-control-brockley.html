Do you cover Brockley?

How much does pest control cost in Brockley?

How quickly can you get to Brockley?

We aim to see urgent problems, such as rats indoors or a wasp nest by a door, as quickly as we can. Call us and we will tell you when we can attend.

Why do flats in Brockley keep getting mice?

Mice move between flats through shared floor voids, risers and pipe boxing. Treating neighbouring flats together and sealing gaps in each one works far better than treating one flat alone.

Can you deal with squirrels or wasps at my house in Brockley?

Yes. We treat wasp nests, usually in one visit, and deal with grey squirrels in lofts legally and humanely before proofing the roof.

Who do I report rats to in Brockley?

If rats are coming from neglected land or a neighbouring property, you can report it to Lewisham Council's environmental health team. For rats in your own home or business, we can inspect and treat.

Do you work with landlords and letting agents in Brockley?

Yes. We arrange access with tenants, treat and proof, and send a written report. See pest control for landlords.
