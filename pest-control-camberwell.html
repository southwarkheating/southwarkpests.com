Local pest control in Camberwell

Homes in Camberwell are mostly Georgian and Victorian houses, many converted into flats, alongside large estates. Converted houses with shared floor voids are ideal for mice, and the takeaways and shops along Camberwell Church Street and Denmark Hill keep rats busy at the back of the high street.

Camberwell is in the London Borough of Southwark, between Peckham, Walworth and Brixton. Camberwell Green sits at the centre, and King’s College Hospital is just up Denmark Hill.

How quickly can you get to Camberwell?

Why do flats in Camberwell keep getting mice?

Mice move between flats through shared floor voids, risers and pipe boxing. Treating neighbouring flats together and sealing gaps in each one works far better than treating one flat alone.

Do you treat restaurants and shops in Camberwell?

Who do I report rats to in Camberwell?

If rats are coming from neglected land or a neighbouring property, you can report it to Southwark Council's environmental health team. For rats in your own home or business, we can inspect and treat.
