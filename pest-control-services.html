How we approach every job

Good pest control is not just a spray or a box of bait. Every visit starts with an inspection to confirm the pest, how far it has spread, how it is getting in and what is attracting it. Then we treat with the right method for that pest and that property, and recommend proofing or changes that stop it coming back.

Prices

A standard visit is £90. Many problems, such as rats, cockroaches and bed bugs, need follow-up visits; wasp nests are usually a single visit. We always explain what is needed and agree it with you first. See prices.

Questions and answers

Do you treat pests I cannot identify?

Yes. Book a visit and choose 'Not sure' as the pest type. We will identify it on site.

Do you deal with bees?

We advise on bees and never treat them unless there is no safe alternative. Honeybee swarms should be collected by a beekeeper.

Do you deal with moles or rabbits?

No. We focus on pests in and around homes and businesses in South London.
