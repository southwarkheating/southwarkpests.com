Local pest control in Catford

Catford is made up largely of Edwardian and 1930s houses and flats around the town centre. Family homes with gardens see wasps, squirrels and rats under sheds, while the town centre's shops and takeaways need protection from mice and rats.

Catford is in the London Borough of Lewisham and is the borough’s civic centre. The Broadway Theatre and the giant cat above the shopping centre are local landmarks.
