Local pest control in Plumstead

Plumstead is made up largely of Victorian and Edwardian terraces, many rented. Older rented terraces see mice and bed bugs, and Plumstead Common and gardens on the hill bring foxes, squirrels and wasps.

Plumstead is in the Royal Borough of Greenwich, east of Woolwich. Plumstead Common gives the area plenty of green space on the hill above the high street.
