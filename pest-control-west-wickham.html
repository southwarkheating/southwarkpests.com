Local pest control in West Wickham

West Wickham is made up largely of 1930s semi-detached houses near the woods. West Wickham Common and the woods nearby bring squirrels, foxes and wasps, and cluster flies gather in lofts in autumn.

West Wickham is in the London Borough of Bromley, near the border with Croydon. West Wickham Common and the surrounding woods give the area a semi-rural edge.
