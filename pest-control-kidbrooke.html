Local pest control in Kidbrooke

Kidbrooke is made up largely of new-build flats and houses at Kidbrooke Village, with older houses nearby. New-build homes often see grey silverfish and pigeons on balconies, while Sutcliffe Park and the river Quaggy bring rats and foxes.

Kidbrooke is in the Royal Borough of Greenwich. Much of the area has been rebuilt as Kidbrooke Village, with new homes beside Sutcliffe Park.
