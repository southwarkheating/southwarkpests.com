How it works

  1. Book online or call. Tell us what you have seen, where and when suits you.
  2. Inspection. We confirm the pest, find how it is getting in and what is attracting it.
  3. Treatment. Safe, targeted treatment using the right method for the pest and your property.
  4. Proofing and follow-up. We seal entry points where needed and come back to check the problem has gone.

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you cover?

South London, Central London (the City, the West End and Westminster), East London out to Stratford and Newham, and nearby Kent. See areas we cover or call 020 8050 2022 with your postcode.

How much does pest control cost?

A standard visit is £90. If your problem needs follow-ups or proofing, we explain what is involved and agree the price with you before we continue.

Which pests do you treat?

Rats, mice, squirrels, cockroaches, bed bugs, fleas, ants, silverfish, spiders, carpet beetles, clothes moths, pantry pests, flies, cluster flies, wasps, hornets, pigeons, gulls and foxes. We advise on bees.

Is pest control safe for children and pets?

Yes, when done properly. We use tamper-resistant bait stations and targeted treatments and tell you about any precautions. Tell us about children and pets when you book.

Will the pests come back?

Not if the cause is dealt with. That is why we look for entry points and attractants and recommend proofing as well as treatment.

Do you work with landlords and businesses?

Yes. We work with landlords, letting agents, managing agents, restaurants, shops, offices and more, with written reports.
