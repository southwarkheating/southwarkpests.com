Moths in kitchen cupboards

Small moths in the kitchen are almost always pantry moths, not clothes moths. The fix starts with finding the packet they came from.

Pantry moth or clothes moth?

Pantry moths, usually the Indian meal moth, are found in kitchens and food cupboards. They are about 10 mm long with wings that are pale grey at the base and copper towards the tip, and they fly readily, especially at dusk. Clothes moths are smaller, plain golden-buff, avoid light and stay near wardrobes and carpets.

Where pantry moths come from

Eggs or larvae are usually already in a product when you bring it home: flour, cereal, rice, dried fruit, nuts, spices, chocolate, bird seed or pet food. They then spread to other open packets.

How to clear them

  1. Empty the cupboard and check every dry food item, including sealed packets, herbs, spices and pet food
  2. Look for webbing, clumps, larvae or small holes in packets
  3. Throw away anything infested in a sealed bag, outside
  4. Vacuum shelves, corners, hinges, shelf-peg holes and the gap under the shelves where larvae pupate
  5. Wipe surfaces down and let them dry
  6. Store all new dry goods in airtight containers
  7. Use a pantry moth pheromone trap to check for remaining moths

Why they keep coming back

If moths reappear, there is a source you have missed. Common culprits are a forgotten bag of bird seed in a shed, dog biscuits in the utility room, or food spilt behind a kitchen unit. Larvae also wander some distance to pupate, so check the ceiling and door frames for cocoons.

Questions and answers

Can I eat food that had moths in it?

Throw away visibly infested food. It is spoiled and unpleasant even if not usually harmful.

Do pantry moths eat clothes?

No. Pantry moths feed on dry foods. If moths are in your wardrobe, see clothes moths.

How long does it take to get rid of pantry moths?

Often a few weeks, as any remaining larvae and pupae finish developing. Keep checking traps.
