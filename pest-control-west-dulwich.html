Local pest control in West Dulwich

West Dulwich is made up largely of family houses with gardens. Belair Park, the railway and long gardens mean squirrels, foxes, wasps and rats under decking and sheds.

West Dulwich is split between the London Boroughs of Lambeth and Southwark. Belair Park, with its Georgian house and lake, sits beside the station.
