Pest control in West Norwood: questions and answers

Do you cover West Norwood?

Yes. We cover West Norwood (SE27) and the surrounding streets from our base at South Dock Marina in SE16. Call 020 8050 2022 or book online.

How much does pest control cost in West Norwood?

A standard visit is £90, the same as everywhere we work. If you need follow-up visits or proofing, we explain the work and agree the price before we continue. See our prices.

How quickly can you get to West Norwood?

We aim to see urgent problems, such as rats indoors or a wasp nest by a door, as quickly as we can. Call us and we will tell you when we can attend.

Why do flats in West Norwood keep getting mice?

Mice move between flats through shared floor voids, risers and pipe boxing. Treating neighbouring flats together and sealing gaps in each one works far better than treating one flat alone.

Can you deal with squirrels or wasps at my house in West Norwood?

Yes. We treat wasp nests, usually in one visit, and deal with grey squirrels in lofts legally and humanely before proofing the roof.

Who do I report rats to in West Norwood?

If rats are coming from neglected land or a neighbouring property, you can report it to Lambeth Council's environmental health team. For rats in your own home or business, we can inspect and treat.

Do you work with landlords and letting agents in West Norwood?

Yes. We arrange access with tenants, treat and proof, and send a written report. See pest control for landlords.
