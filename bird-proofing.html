Bird proofing in South London

Bird proofing stops pigeons and gulls landing, roosting and nesting where they cause damage and mess. It is humane, legal and lasts for years when fitted properly.

Where we fit bird proofing

  • Balconies in flats, especially in taller blocks
  • Under solar panels, where pigeons nest out of sight
  • Window ledges, parapets and roof edges
  • Shop signs, canopies and fascias
  • Loading bays, warehouses and plant rooms
  • Chimney stacks and flat roofs used by gulls

Proofing methods

Netting

High-quality bird netting fitted on a tensioned frame closes off a balcony, light well, courtyard or roof void completely. It is the most reliable option where birds are nesting.

Spikes

Stainless steel or polycarbonate spikes stop birds landing on ledges, signs and beams. They do not harm the birds; they simply leave no flat landing space.

Post and wire systems

Thin, tensioned wires on low posts make ledges and parapets uncomfortable to land on, with very little visual impact on the building.

Solar panel mesh

A galvanised or coated mesh clipped around the edge of solar panels, without drilling the panels, keeps pigeons from nesting underneath.

Clean-up of fouling

Pigeon droppings are acidic, slippery and can carry bacteria and fungal spores. Before proofing we clear nests and droppings safely, using protective equipment, and disinfect the area.

The law on wild birds

All wild birds, their active nests and eggs are protected under the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981. Proofing is the right approach because it prevents nesting rather than harming birds. Where an active nest is present, we plan the work around it.

Getting a quote

Most bird proofing is quoted after a survey because the right method depends on the building and access. Send us photos through the contact form or call 020 8050 2022.

Questions and answers

Does netting harm birds?

No. Properly fitted, tensioned netting keeps birds out without trapping them. We check that no birds are inside before closing it.

Will spikes work for gulls?

Gulls are larger and need different products. We use gull-rated spikes, wires or netting on roofs and chimneys.

Can you proof solar panels without damaging them?

Yes. Solar panel mesh clips to the panel frame without drilling into the panels.

Do I need permission to fit netting on a balcony?

If you rent or own a leasehold flat, check your lease or ask your managing agent first. Listed buildings may also need consent.
