Pest control across the borough

The Royal Borough of Greenwich runs along the Thames from Greenwich and the Peninsula through Charlton, Woolwich and Thamesmead, and inland to Blackheath, Kidbrooke, Eltham, Plumstead and Shooters Hill. New riverside developments sit alongside Georgian terraces, 1930s suburbs and large estates.

Pests we treat most in Greenwich

  • Rats along the river, in Thamesmead's lakes and canals, and in gardens
  • Mice in older terraces and blocks
  • Pigeons and gulls on riverside apartment blocks
  • Grey silverfish in new-build flats on the Peninsula, at Kidbrooke Village and in Woolwich
  • Squirrels, foxes, wasps and cluster flies around Greenwich Park, Blackheath, Oxleas Wood and Eltham

Greenwich businesses

Greenwich Market, Woolwich town centre and the riverside's restaurants and bars need dependable, documented pest control. See commercial pest control.

Reporting pests to the council

Report rats on neglected land or from a neighbouring property to the Royal Borough of Greenwich's environmental health team. For private treatment, book a visit.
