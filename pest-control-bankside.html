Local pest control in Bankside

Most of Bankside is modern riverside blocks and older purpose-built flats, many of them rented. Riverside buildings, restaurants and offices bring rats from the river wall and drains, while pigeons and gulls use balconies and roofs.

Bankside is the riverside part of the London Borough of Southwark between Blackfriars and London Bridge. Tate Modern and Shakespeare’s Globe sit on the river here, with homes in the streets just behind.

Why are there so many rats near the river in Bankside?

The river wall, docks and old drain runs give rats food, water and shelter. Most rat problems in riverside homes start in defective drains, so we check drains and fit rat blockers where suitable.

Who do I report rats to in Bankside?

If rats are coming from neglected land or a neighbouring property, you can report it to Southwark Council's environmental health team. For rats in your own home or business, we can inspect and treat.

Do you work with landlords and letting agents in Bankside?

Yes. We arrange access with tenants, treat and proof, and send a written report. See pest control for landlords.
