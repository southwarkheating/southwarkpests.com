Local pest control in Beckenham

Beckenham is made up largely of Victorian and Edwardian houses, 1930s family homes and flats. Kelsey Park, the railway lines and big gardens bring squirrels, foxes, rats and wasps, and older houses see mice in winter.

Beckenham is in the London Borough of Bromley. Kelsey Park, with its lake and woodland, is a short walk from the high street.
