Local pest control in Nunhead

Nunhead is made up largely of Victorian and Edwardian terraces with a village feel. Back gardens running to Nunhead Cemetery and the railway are home to foxes and rats, and older terraces give mice plenty of routes between houses.

Nunhead is in the London Borough of Southwark, just south-east of Peckham. Nunhead Cemetery, one of London’s “Magnificent Seven” Victorian cemeteries, is a local landmark.
