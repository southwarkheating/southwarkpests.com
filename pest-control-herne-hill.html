Local pest control in Herne Hill

Herne Hill is made up largely of Victorian and Edwardian family houses, some converted to flats. Brockwell Park and big gardens bring squirrels, foxes and wasps, and the railway line is a highway for rats.

Herne Hill is split between the London Boroughs of Lambeth and Southwark. Brockwell Park, with its 1930s lido, is right beside the station.
