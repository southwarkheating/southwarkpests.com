Local pest control in Clapham

Homes in Clapham are mostly Victorian terraces, many split into flats and rented. Rented conversions see mice and bed bugs, the high street's restaurants need regular pest control, and Clapham Common brings foxes and squirrels.

Clapham is mostly in the London Borough of Lambeth, with its western side in Wandsworth. (Clapham Junction station is actually in Battersea.) Clapham Common, one of South London’s best-known open spaces, sits at its centre.

Pest control in Clapham: questions and answers

Do you cover Clapham?

Yes. We cover Clapham (SW4) and the surrounding streets from our base at South Dock Marina in SE16. Call 020 8050 2022 or book online.

How much does pest control cost in Clapham?

A standard visit is £90, the same as everywhere we work. If you need follow-up visits or proofing, we explain the work and agree the price before we continue. See our prices.

How quickly can you get to Clapham?

We aim to see urgent problems, such as rats indoors or a wasp nest by a door, as quickly as we can. Call us and we will tell you when we can attend.

Why do flats in Clapham keep getting mice?

Mice move between flats through shared floor voids, risers and pipe boxing. Treating neighbouring flats together and sealing gaps in each one works far better than treating one flat alone.

Can you deal with squirrels or wasps at my house in Clapham?

Yes. We treat wasp nests, usually in one visit, and deal with grey squirrels in lofts legally and humanely before proofing the roof.

Do you treat restaurants and shops in Clapham?

Yes. We work with food businesses, shops and offices, with written reports for your food safety records. See commercial pest control.

Who do I report rats to in Clapham?

If rats are coming from neglected land or a neighbouring property, you can report it to Lambeth Council's environmental health team. For rats in your own home or business, we can inspect and treat.
