Commercial pest control

Warehouse pest control in South London

Warehouses have big doors, lots of deliveries and stock that sits for weeks. That makes them attractive to rodents, birds and stored product insects. We protect industrial units across South London and north Kent.

Common warehouse pest problems

  • Rats and mice entering through loading bay doors, gaps under roller shutters and damaged cladding
  • Pigeons roosting in roof trusses and fouling stock and racking
  • Stored product insects in food, grain, animal feed and packaging
  • Flies and wasps around waste compactors and yards

Industrial estates

From Old Kent Road and Bermondsey trading estates to Charlton, Belvedere, Erith and Crayford, industrial areas often sit beside railways, the river and open land, which brings pressure from rats. A perimeter of external bait stations, internal monitoring traps and well-maintained doors is the backbone of warehouse pest control.

Our approach

  1. Site survey and plan showing external and internal monitoring points
  2. External bait stations around the perimeter and yard
  3. Internal monitoring with traps and insect monitors near doors, racking and stock
  4. Proofing: door brushes, roller shutter seals, mesh on vents and repairs to damaged cladding
  5. Bird proofing of roof spaces with netting or spikes where pigeons roost
  6. Reports after each visit for audits, customers and insurers

Audits and customer requirements

Many warehouse customers, especially in food, require documented pest control. Our records show the site plan, monitoring results, treatments and recommendations.

Questions and answers

Do you work on industrial estates outside London?

We cover South London and nearby parts of north Kent. Call to check your postcode.

Can you net a warehouse roof?

Yes. We survey the roof space and quote for netting or other bird proofing suited to the structure.

Can you provide a site plan?

Yes. Contract customers receive a site plan showing every monitoring point.
