Local pest control in East Dulwich

Most of East Dulwich is Victorian and Edwardian terraces, many split into upstairs and downstairs flats. Mice travel easily between flats in converted terraces, and the cafés and restaurants along Lordship Lane need regular pest control to protect their kitchens.

East Dulwich is in the London Borough of Southwark, between Peckham and Dulwich Village. Lordship Lane runs through the middle, lined with independent shops and cafes.

Pest control in East Dulwich: questions and answers

Do you cover East Dulwich?

Yes. We cover East Dulwich (SE22) and the surrounding streets from our base at South Dock Marina in SE16. Call 020 8050 2022 or book online.

How much does pest control cost in East Dulwich?

A standard visit is £90, the same as everywhere we work. If you need follow-up visits or proofing, we explain the work and agree the price before we continue. See our prices.

How quickly can you get to East Dulwich?

We aim to see urgent problems, such as rats indoors or a wasp nest by a door, as quickly as we can. Call us and we will tell you when we can attend.

Why do flats in East Dulwich keep getting mice?

Mice move between flats through shared floor voids, risers and pipe boxing. Treating neighbouring flats together and sealing gaps in each one works far better than treating one flat alone.

Can you deal with squirrels or wasps at my house in East Dulwich?

Yes. We treat wasp nests, usually in one visit, and deal with grey squirrels in lofts legally and humanely before proofing the roof.

Do you treat restaurants and shops in East Dulwich?

Yes. We work with food businesses, shops and offices, with written reports for your food safety records. See commercial pest control.

Who do I report rats to in East Dulwich?

If rats are coming from neglected land or a neighbouring property, you can report it to Southwark Council's environmental health team. For rats in your own home or business, we can inspect and treat.
