Local pest control in Bermondsey

Bermondsey is made up largely of warehouse conversions by the river, Victorian terraces and estate flats. Old warehouse buildings, railway arches and the food businesses around Bermondsey Street and Maltby Street mean rats and mice are never far away, and estate bin stores are a common trouble spot.

Bermondsey is in the London Borough of Southwark, just east of London Bridge. Bermondsey Street and the food stalls around Maltby Street draw people from all over London, but most of the area is homes.

Pest control in Bermondsey: questions and answers

Do you cover Bermondsey?

Yes. We cover Bermondsey (SE1 and SE16) and the surrounding streets from our base at South Dock Marina in SE16. Call 020 8050 2022 or book online.

How much does pest control cost in Bermondsey?

A standard visit is £90, the same as everywhere we work. If you need follow-up visits or proofing, we explain the work and agree the price before we continue. See our prices.

How quickly can you get to Bermondsey?

We aim to see urgent problems, such as rats indoors or a wasp nest by a door, as quickly as we can. Call us and we will tell you when we can attend.

Why do flats in Bermondsey keep getting mice?

Mice move between flats through shared floor voids, risers and pipe boxing. Treating neighbouring flats together and sealing gaps in each one works far better than treating one flat alone.

Do you treat restaurants and shops in Bermondsey?

Yes. We work with food businesses, shops and offices, with written reports for your food safety records. See commercial pest control.

Why are there so many rats near the river in Bermondsey?

The river wall, docks and old drain runs give rats food, water and shelter. Most rat problems in riverside homes start in defective drains, so we check drains and fit rat blockers where suitable.

Who do I report rats to in Bermondsey?

If rats are coming from neglected land or a neighbouring property, you can report it to Southwark Council's environmental health team. For rats in your own home or business, we can inspect and treat.
