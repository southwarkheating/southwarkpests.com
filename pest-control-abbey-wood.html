Local pest control in Abbey Wood

Most of Abbey Wood is post-war houses and newer flats close to the Elizabeth line station. Lesnes Abbey Woods and the open land towards Thamesmead mean rats, foxes and wasps in gardens, and new blocks bring pigeons and grey silverfish.

Abbey Wood is split between the Royal Borough of Greenwich and the London Borough of Bexley. Lesnes Abbey Woods and the ruins of Lesnes Abbey are on the Bexley side.
