Sidcup is made up largely of 1930s family houses and some flats. Lamorbey Park, Foots Cray Meadows and long gardens bring squirrels, foxes and wasps, and cluster flies in autumn.

Sidcup is in the London Borough of Bexley. Lamorbey Park, with its lake and historic house, is in the north of the area.
