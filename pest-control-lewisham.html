Pest control across the borough

Lewisham borough mixes riverside regeneration in Deptford, student houses in New Cross, big Victorian houses in Brockley and Forest Hill, Edwardian terraces in Hither Green and Catford, and new blocks around Lewisham town centre. Each brings its own pests.

Pests we treat most in Lewisham

  • Mice in converted Victorian houses and shared student homes
  • Rats around Deptford High Street, the creek, the Ravensbourne and railway lines
  • Bed bugs in shared houses and rented flats
  • Squirrels, foxes and wasps around Hilly Fields, the Horniman, Blythe Hill Fields and Sydenham Hill Wood
  • Pigeons on balconies of new blocks around Lewisham station

Lewisham businesses

Deptford Market, Lewisham Market and the high streets of Catford, Forest Hill and Sydenham have many food businesses that need regular, recorded pest control. See commercial pest control.

Reporting pests to the council

Report rats on neglected land or from a neighbouring property to Lewisham Council's environmental health team. For private treatment, book a visit.
