Local pest control in Lewisham

Property in Lewisham is mostly new apartment blocks around the town centre and older terraces in the streets beyond. The market, shopping centre and new blocks around the station see pigeons, mice and bed bugs, and the River Ravensbourne and Quaggy bring rats into nearby gardens.

Lewisham town centre is the heart of the London Borough of Lewisham. The town centre is home to Lewisham Shopping Centre and a long-running street market, with many new homes built around the station.

Why are there so many rats near the river in Lewisham?

The river wall, docks and old drain runs give rats food, water and shelter. Most rat problems in riverside homes start in defective drains, so we check drains and fit rat blockers where suitable.

Who do I report rats to in Lewisham?

If rats are coming from neglected land or a neighbouring property, you can report it to Lewisham Council's environmental health team. For rats in your own home or business, we can inspect and treat.

Yes. We arrange access with tenants, treat and proof, and send a written report. See pest control for landlords.
